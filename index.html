      Settings

      Location

      Choose where you want roles to be. Pick both to see New York and remote roles together. Remote means remote roles not tied to another country. The daily refresh only keeps New York and remote roles that meet your salary floor (set in filters.json on GitHub), so widening these settings cannot bring back roles it has already dropped.

      Salary

      Job types

      Keywords matched against the title, location and company. Separate alternatives with commas. Words in one group must all appear.

      Match

      Each role gets a 1 to 5 score from an AI estimate of how well it fits your skills profile, added by the daily refresh. The score and its one-line reason show under each role. It is a guide, not a verdict, so open the posting before you rule anything out.
